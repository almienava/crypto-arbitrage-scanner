# Crypto Arbitrage Scanner (Arbitradar.io)

**Arbitradar.io** is a real-time **crypto arbitrage scanner** and dashboard that helps you **find, evaluate, and act on price spreads** across multiple exchanges—fast.

- 🔍 **Scan 11+ exchanges** in real time  
- 🧠 **AI Confidence Score** to prioritize the most reliable spreads  
- ⚡ **Instant Telegram alerts** so you act before the window closes  
- 🎯 **Custom filters** (exchange, pair, spread %, volume)  
- 📈 Optional trade logging to track outcomes over time

> **Goal:** Make building an arbitrage strategy **simple, fast, and repeatable**.

## 📚 FAQ

**Is arbitrage risk-free?**  
No. Fees, latency, slippage, withdrawal limits, and liquidity can affect outcomes.

**Does AI Confidence guarantee profit?**  
No. It’s a **prioritization tool** to reduce noise and surface historically more reliable signals.

**Which exchanges are supported?**  
Major centralized exchanges like **Binance, OKX, KuCoin, MEXC**, and others. The list may grow over time.

**Do I need a bot?**  
No. Many users execute manually from alerts. Exports are available if you want to automate.
